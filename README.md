# Fitted – online shop prototype

Clickable UI prototype for a fit-first online shop, built for our training project.

**Persona:** 55, real estate manager, three children, little time. Shops online but returns a lot because of size, taste and quality, and wants to stay on trend.

## Screens
- **For you** – recommendations based on the style quiz, past purchases and returns, with a fit check on every item
- **Product page** – garment drawn against her body outline with cm of room per zone, size advice, quality facts, studio vs. daylight colour
- **Fitting room** – avatar built from her measurements; dress it and see tight/comfortable/roomy fit lines
- **Inspiration** – Pinterest / Instagram looks matched to pieces in her size, "try on me" and "add look"
- **Stylist** – chatbot that uses her quiz answers, measurements and order history
- **Style quiz** – 5-step onboarding (looks, colours, fit preferences, measurements, week & budget)
- **Fit profile** – editable measurements, return reasons and the rules learned from them

## Run
Open `fitted-prototype.html` in a browser. No build step.

All persona, product, creator and statistics data is sample data. Integrations and the chatbot are simulated.
